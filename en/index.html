Ideario

Speak or type. Ideario sorts it out.

Ideario turns what you dictate or type into Calendar events, Reminders tasks, and notes, so you can review them and send them in one tap. It works on iPhone and Apple Watch.

Have a question, a problem, or a suggestion? Email josmen.app.support@icloud.com. If you can, include the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and your iPhone model.

Frequently asked questions

Why can’t Ideario save notes straight to Notes?
Apple doesn’t let other apps create notes. Ideario prepares them, and when you tap “Save to Notes” it opens the share sheet so you can choose Notes.

Why does it ask for full access to Reminders?
iOS doesn’t offer add-only access to reminders. Ideario only uses it to add the tasks you send and to show your lists.

Does it read my calendar events?
No. Ideario can only add events. They go to your default calendar, which you can change in Settings › Apps › Calendar.

What is “basic mode”?
When Apple Intelligence is available, Ideario uses it on your device to organize your text. Otherwise, it uses a rule-based organizer that also works offline.

It couldn’t record. What should I do?
Check in Settings › Apps › Ideario that the microphone is allowed. The first time, it may need to download the speech model for your language. In the meantime, you can type.

Ideas from Apple Watch don’t reach my iPhone.
They’re sent when your iPhone is nearby and paired. When you open Ideario on your iPhone, they appear on the home screen, ready to organize.
