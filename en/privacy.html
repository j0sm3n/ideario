Privacy Policy

App: Ideario (iPhone and Apple Watch)
Controller: Jose Antonio Mendoza Rodriguez
Contact: josmen.app.support@icloud.com
Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Summary

Ideario doesn’t collect, send, or share personal data. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools. Everything you capture is processed and stored on your device.

2. What data the app handles and where it’s stored

  • What you dictate or type and the items it’s organized into (events, tasks, and notes). It’s stored only on your iPhone, in the app’s history, and isn’t synced with any service.
  • Your voice. Dictation is transcribed on your device with Apple’s speech recognition. Audio is never recorded or sent.
  • Organizing your text. When available, Apple Intelligence is used on your device. Otherwise, a rule-based organizer that works offline is used.
  • Apple Watch. Ideas you dictate on your watch are sent to your iPhone through the direct connection between your Apple devices.
  • Settings (daily reminder, Reminders list, haptics). Stored on your device.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone: to dictate.
  • Calendar (add only): to create the events you send. Ideario can’t read your events.
  • Reminders: to add the tasks you send and show your lists. iOS requires full access for this, but Ideario doesn’t read or change your existing reminders.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the daily reminder.

Notes are saved through the system share sheet, so you choose where they go.

4. In-app purchases

Optional tips are paid through Apple. Ideario doesn’t receive or store any payment information. Purchases are handled under Apple’s privacy policy.

5. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual captures or your whole history in the app, and deleting the app removes all of its data. Anything you’ve already sent to Calendar, Reminders, or Notes stays in those apps and is managed there.

6. Children

Ideario isn’t specifically directed at children and doesn’t collect data from anyone.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.

8. Your rights

Because Ideario doesn’t collect personal data, the controller holds no data about you. For any question, email the contact address above. You can also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency: aepd.es.
